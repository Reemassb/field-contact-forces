How to complete this activity

  1. Type your name and grade at the top.
  2. Read each scene. The letter on each orange arrow matches a force below it.
  3. For every force, choose its type and whether it is field or contact.
  4. Use Check my answers to see which boxes are right (limited checks).
  5. Download your PDF and submit it to your teacher.
Contact force

Acts only when the two objects are touching.

Field force

Acts across a distance through a field. The objects do not need to touch. Also called a non-contact force.
